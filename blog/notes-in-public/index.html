Notes, in public

17 August 2026

I keep a lot of notes. Most of them live in run logs, analysis notebooks and lesson files that only make sense sitting next to the data they came from. This page is for the parts that generalise: results from the Josephson-junction-array campaign, what the nanoSQUID work taught me about pulling a small signal out of a noisy system, and what actually happens when an AI analyst does real analysis alongside me.

The bar for a post here is that it says something I checked. Where a claim rests on data I will name the run it came from, and where I am guessing I will say so.
